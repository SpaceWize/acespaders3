Four steps.
No surprises.

  1. 01

    30 Minute assesment

    All meetings and commitments start by speaking with Ace’s assistant. Explain your situation and give an overview of what you need from Ace.

  2. 02

    Approval

    After your assessment call, Ace will review your request. If he agrees to the scope of work, his assistant will provide you with a quote and agreement.

  3. 03

    Orientation

    Once the agreement is signed and initial payment is made, you will be scheduled for a 60 minute meeting with Ace to discuss your situation and/or potential next steps.

  4. 04

    Action

    Meetings are typically 60 minutes but may be longer, subject to Ace’s discretion. Commitments are ongoing and subject to the scope of work agreed upon.

Who you’re hiring

Matthew Ferguson.
Most people call
him Ace.

Spending close to two decades being handed things that needed fixing, a guiding hand, or a complete overhaul, Matthew has developed a perspective that allows him to keep moving forward where others would give up.

As a husband, father of three, and business owner, Matthew knows how to ask the right questions, at the right times, and adapt to the needs in front of him. He doesn’t just see the problems others ignore - he can clearly recognize, or find out, why those problems exist and determine the best way, if not multiple ways, to resolve them.

Speak with him or meet him once, and you’ll most likely walk away with something. Whether it’s a new perspective, a clear mind, some renewed motivation or even actionable steps towards something you’re working on, there is lots he can offer in a short meeting. Commit to working with him consistently towards a clear goal though, and you have a wealth of knowledge, life experience, connections, and unwavering determination at your disposal.
