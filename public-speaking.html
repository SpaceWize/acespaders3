Public Speaking

Ace is available for keynotes and talks focused on resilience, personal development, and the practical benefits of networking. He can also speak on nearly any topic required when he is given some preparation time, and information on the topic.

He is selective about what he takes on and prefers situations where he is aligned with the message so that he can ensure it lands with the room.

In addition to formal speaking engagements, Ace hosts the All Aces Networking Event once every three months. At these gatherings he opens with a short talk (15 minutes maximum) before facilitating conversation and connection, usually over a meal. These are structured networking events with a brief opening talk from Ace, not formal keynotes.

The regular Group Activities he runs throughout the month are facilitated sessions for fostering connection around shared interests for business and family leaders, not as speaking events.

The Invested Aces Mastermind allows you to speak with Ace on a more personal level, while having access to the All Aces Networking Events and Group Activities through your membership.

The best way to decide if Ace is the public speaker you’re seeking, is to spend time with him through his established avenues of connection, or reach out immediately to secure your time on his calendar.
