Invested Aces Mastermind

The Invested Aces Mastermind is a private, application-only group for people who want consistent proximity to Ace and a vetted circle of others who take their own progress seriously. Members receive access to monthly Mastermind meetings, Ace’s group activities, and increasing levels of direct engagement with Ace depending on the tier they choose.

Membership runs for 12 months and is renewable. Membership fees can be paid monthly or annually. All tiers require an application and approval.

Current rates are founding member pricing. As new group activities are launched, membership pricing will increase to reflect the added value. Members who join now lock in their rate for the duration of their agreement.

$1,000 / month

Member Benefits:

  • Access to one of the two monthly Mastermind meetings
  • Complimentary access to Ace’s Group Activities (first-priority RSVP for Poker Night)
  • Access to Premium Pickup & Transport at the $200/h member rate

Best for: People who primarily want to be part of the network, attend group activities, and take part in one Mastermind meeting each month.

$2,000 / month

Member Benefits:

  • All $1000 benefits, plus
  • Access to one or both monthly Mastermind meetings
  • Up to two one-on-one meetings with Ace per month (typically 1 hour each, longer at Ace’s discretion - does not carry over if availability fills up)
  • Complimentary Premium Pickup & Transport once per month (Lower Mainland only, maximum 3 hours, single-day use). Additional time is billed at the member rate of $200/hour. Terms and conditions apply for additional distance or frequency.
  • One-on-one meetings and transport requests are scheduled through Ace’s Assistant

Best for: People who want regular group access plus consistent direct time with Ace.

$4,000 / month

  • All $2000 benefits, plus
  • Up to two on-site visits per month to the member’s place of business for assessments or requested support (does carry over if availability fills up)
  • First priority when scheduling one-on-ones and on-site visits

Best for: People who want the highest level of direct access and practical, on-the-ground support from Ace.

Mastermind Meeting Details

  • Meetings are held on the 2nd Tuesday and 3rd Saturday of each month
  • Locations vary and are kept private. Exact details are shared by email two weeks in advance

A private Mastermind group chat is in development for members.

Group Activities & RSVP

Members receive links to all group activities and must RSVP directly to Ace or his Assistant to request a ticket (aside from Aces Poker Night). Free member tickets are issued only after an RSVP and are not visible to the public. Attendance without a confirmed RSVP is not permitted.

RSVP for Aces Poker Night is free for members and non-members, with the understanding that an RSVP means an agreement to a minimum $100 buy-in. Members get first priority for RSVPs.

Full attendance and RSVP expectations are outlined in the Ace Community Code of Conduct.

Application Process

  • Submit an application.
  • Pay the $200 application fee (this covers a 1-hour meeting with Ace).
  • Attend the meeting so Ace can determine fit.
  • If approved, the $200 is credited toward your first month’s membership. If not approved, the fee is non-refundable.

Approval typically takes 2-5 days, depending on meeting scheduling.

All approved members will sign a membership agreement and be sent the Ace Community Code of Conduct to acknowledge before starting.

Who This Is For

People who value direct conversation, personal responsibility, and being around others who are actively investing in themselves. Location in or near the Lower Mainland of BC is strongly preferred due to the in-person nature of the meetings and activities.

Who This Is Not For

This is not for people looking for passive content, large unstructured networking events, or a place to promote their products and services. It is also not for those who are not prepared to show up consistently and contribute to the quality of the room.
