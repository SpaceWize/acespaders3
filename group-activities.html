Ace’s Group Activities

Ace’s Group Activities are regular, in-person gatherings built around shared interests, conversation, and real connection. Complimentary access to most of these activities is included in the Invested Aces Mastermind Memberships, as well as first priority to RSVP for others. All are available for individual RSVP drop-ins, but a higher rate for non-members.

These activities are for people who want to spend time with Ace and others who value direct conversation, personal responsibility, and forward movement. They are not networking events solely for the sake of collecting contacts, or selling your products and services. The expectation is simple: show up ready to participate, respect the room, and contribute to the quality of the time spent together and the insights shared.

What people can expect varies by activity, but the core concept is consistent: practical conversation, shared experience, and proximity to Ace and other members of the network. Some activities are more physical, some more social or strategic, and a few combine both.

All attendees will be emailed the Ace Community Code of Conduct. Anyone found in violation will be asked to leave.

Currently Available Activities

  • Aces Running Group (on the 2nd Saturday of each month)
  • Aces Poker Night (on the 3rd Wednesday of each month)
  • All Aces Networking Events (once every 3 months)

Activities in Development

The following activities are not yet running but will be rolled out over time as the Invested Aces Mastermind expands and demand becomes clearer. When new activities are added, membership pricing will be updated to reflect the increased benefits. Existing members will continue at their current rate, as outlined in their agreement.

  • All Aces VIP Mixer (Vancouver)
  • Aces Trading Club
  • Aces Gun Club (shooting range days)
  • The Claude Club
  • Aces Golf Club (driving range days and full game days)

Joining the Invested Aces Mastermind now locks in founding pricing. Early members’ feedback helps refine the activities, and their interest can help determine which ones launch first, or if new ones get added.
